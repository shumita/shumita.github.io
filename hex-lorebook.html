War of the Holy Beast

[COUNTRY THAT WE WILL NAME LATER], split into three kingdoms due to disagreements on how to rule.
Each kingdom worshiped an ethereal spirit called a Holy beast. The Holy Beasts would each choose a vessel that embodied their aspect
The first Kingdom worshiped the Fenghuang (Phoenix), with its emperor wanting to rule passionately above all else.
The second kingdom worshiped the Dragon, with its emperor wanting to rule with authoritarian power.
The third worshiped the Qilin, with its emperor wanting to rule with kindness toward other countries/kingdoms.
Each emperor was a vessel for one of the holy beasts
The fourth Turtle vessel was nowhere to be found because they wanted to preserve their own life, they became the vessel after almost dying and being horrified after seeing the god of Death
La Huesera is found in a small village sitting under a tree motionless, but with a heartbeat
She Becomes a fascinating oddity in the village
The village is raided by one of the Dragon Kingdom’s Armies, and a soldier tries to kill La Heusera
La Heusera wakes up for a short time, yelling out “Hexplosion” and killing the soldier. She then goes back to her vegetative state
The Army bails and the villagers form a cult around La Huesera
They believed she was a god that had fallen from the heavens causing her vegetative state
The cult found sacrifices sending them to attack La Huesera only to die from one of her Hexes
Through trial and error, the cult learns that Hexes are the bones in her body, and each one has a specific ability relating to a pun name (i.e. Hexplosion, Hexelerate, Hexistential) and only activates when the name is said aloud. Hexes flow with some sort of unknown type of magic, but use up La Huesera’s Chi when used
They theorize that the Magic in her Hexes could potentially be able to revive a normal human from the dead, the cult would put her under a gas anesthetic and remove a pinky bone to test the theory
They placed the Hex in a dead body and the Magic in the Hex would bring the dead human back as a Skeleton, however, the skeleton would fall apart if the Hex was removed
Eventually, La Huesera would die under mysterious circumstances
They learn that hexes could Hypothetically The Cult would take out her bones splitting them up between the members and disbanding
The only Hex that wouldn’t be distributed and instead hidden was the Wish Bone (real name Hexplopit), this would become a legend that would last for thousands of years
The war would peter out, with all three vessels dead, and heavy casualties on both sides

Dawn of the Hex

Skip a couple of thousand years later and [COUNTRY THAT WE WILL NAME LATER] became industrialized having four different Zones(or prefecture, areas, or whatnot)
The city in the middle is surrounded by the desert zone, Snow zone, and Grassland Zone (with a lot of hills and rivers)
Empire/government is still pretty unstable, leading to Gangs
The Government has labeled these gangsters as Outlaws, spreading the rumor that anyone participating in gang activity are reincarnated demons
Hexes have been disbursed around the Country mostly being used by outlaws
Holy Beasts and how to become a vessel have mostly been forgotten
The only people who still believe in holy beasts are the Nine Sons cult, descendants of the Dragon Kingdom who believe that Hexes are evil. Instead, they practice honing their chi. They plan to take over the country and rule it the way the Dragon Emperor would have wanted.
The Turtle vessel is still alive due to their power to steal people's life energy. They are planning to take over the country to prevent another war so they can live for as long as possible
The Government created the Devil’s Union, a secret organization made to hunt down chupacabra using scientifically created Hexes, called a Black Hex
Chupacabra are monsters that eat sins. They start in a basic monstrous form, typically going for either people with high amounts of sin or Goats, due to their weird trait of absorbing human sins. They then evolve into a humanoid (but still monstrous) second stage and begin to hunt even more humans. It is unknown what their goals are or where they came from
Most people found by the Devil Union are people whose lives were affected by chupacabra
Caishen’s Grandmother(Panlong Mei formerly known as Fang Mei) and Grandfather(Fang something) do research in the Grassland Zone to find the secret behind the Fenghuang Holy Beast
Grandpa becomes the vessel after dedicated monk training and lines his two sons as his successors
The passion of the Fenghuang goes to his head and it warps into greed, and he becomes a local tyrant with his sons as his lackeys
One of his sons leaves Mei with a Baby Caishen Mei, Terrified of her husband creates robot arm weapons with her engineering knowledge
Grandpa cheats on Mei, causing her breaking point She challenges her family at a lake near their home killing both her sons and her Husband while a baby Caishen was accidentally watching
After the fight, Mei would become the local legend, La Llorona
A couple of years later Axel, a street racer from the city zone, uses his Hexelerate mid-race crashing his car into another racer leading to his arm being blown off in the explosion. He ends up winning the race with a torn-off arm unintentionally becoming a feared figure in the city
Axel sulks for a while since he can’t race with one arm
Lala, Axel’s friend/former racing crew member, tells Axel about the rumors of the fight between La Llarona and they travel to the hilly zone to get him a robot arm
Mei gives Caishen to Axel to take care of her because Mei is scared that living in the Grassland Zone and being raised by Mei will cause the FengHuang to choose Caishen as it’s the next vessel. She hides her reasoning behind Axel needing to learn that his life is valuable through having someone he cares for
Axel Begrudgingly accepts and goes back to the city
Axel works his ass off to buy another car but decides not to because no one would be able to take care of Caishen (other than maybe Lala, but she's busy being a luchador)
Caishen spends most of her time hanging out with her friend Rosita Huesos
Rosita dies after standing up to an outlaw for being mean, the outlaw uses his Hex and kills her Caishen runs away from home. After a couple of days, Caishen comes back home covered in blood and bruises revealing that she beat the outlaw and took his Hex. Her eyes glow a red/Orange color.
She puts the Hex in Rosita bringing her back as a Skeleton. Caishen’s eyes are no longer Red. Caishen declares she’ll find the Wish Bone to make Rosita human again. She’d eventually find her own Hex, Hextend
A couple of years later, Axel converts his home into a bar and forms the Moon Rabbit Bounty Hunters in response to what happened to Rosita. Rosita and Caishen are the first members, followed by Yao Yuze (nicknamed Rudolph due to her Red Nose) the Hexplosion user, Isa Huang the Hexistential user, and Teng Lihua the Hexchange user
